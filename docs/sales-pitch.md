# Mehfil / Settle — Sales Playbook

*Field-ready. What to say to cafe & restaurant owners, how to attract them, how to close. Lead with Settle (the validated wedge), not "POS" or "AI."*

---

## The core rule

**Sell money, not software.** Never open with "POS," "AI," or "platform." Open with: *"I'll show you the money Zomato/Swiggy is quietly taking from you."* Money gets the meeting. Software gets the door shut.

---

## The hook (what attracts them)

The magnet is a **free, risk-free audit**:

> *"Give me your last Zomato/Swiggy statement. I'll show you for free how much they overcharged you. You only pay when I recover it."*

Why it works: zero risk for them, specific (money, not features), and it forces them to hand over data — your foot in the door.

---

## How to find & attract them

1. **Build the list** — walk your zone (East Delhi + Noida border: Sector 15/16/18, Mayur Vihar, Patparganj, Preet Vihar). List 40-60 cafes: name, phone, owner if known.
2. **Timing is everything** — approach **only 3-5 PM**. Never lunch (12-3) or dinner (7-11). You'll get thrown out during rush.
3. **Become a regular first** — at your top 8 targets, buy a coffee a few times. A familiar face converts 5× better than a cold stranger.
4. **The free audit is the opener** — it's the reason to talk to you. Everything flows from "let me check your statement."
5. **Referrals, but don't over-rely** — restaurant owners rarely refer competitors (proven in research). Ask happy owners for *non-competing* intros (a friend in a different area).

## What to say — scripts

### WhatsApp cold message
> Namaste [name] ji 🙏 Main Nikhil.
> Cafe owners ke liye ek free help — Zomato/Swiggy aapse jo *extra* paisa kaat lete hain (ads jo approve nahi kiye, hidden discounts, galat commission) — main aapki last statement dekh ke woh nikaal deta hu. Bilkul free.
> Last month ki Zomato/Swiggy payout report bhej do — aaj check kar deta hu.

### Walk-in opener (3-5 PM)
> "[name] ji, bas 2 minute. Main aas-paas ke cafes ki Zomato-Swiggy statements free mein check kar raha hu — zyadatar se ads aur discounts ke naam pe paisa kat raha hota hai jo owner ne approve bhi nahi kiya. Aapki last statement nikaal sakte hain? Abhi dikha deta hu."

### The 30-second pitch
> "Zomato-Swiggy har mahine ads aur discounts ke naam pe paisa kaat lete hain — aksar woh jo aapne approve bhi nahi kiya. Main woh paisa dhoond ke wapas dilata hu. Free check, phir ₹1,500/month. 5 minute mein aapki last statement pe dikha deta hu — try karein?"

### The value reveal (after you run the audit)
> "Dekho — pichhle mahine Zomato ne ₹14,200 kaata. Isme se ~₹3,000 aisa hai jo aapne approve nahi kiya — yeh wapas mil sakta hai. Main dispute file kar deta hu, aur har mahine automatically check karta rahunga — ₹1,500/month."

## Handling objections

**"Mere paas already Petpooja/POS hai."**
> "Petpooja billing karta hai. Yeh alag cheez hai — yeh aapka *kaata hua paisa wapas* dilata hai. Dono saath chal sakte hain. Koi POS badalne ki zaroorat nahi."

**"Kitna paisa milega?"** *(be honest — don't over-promise)*
> "Har cafe alag hai. Kuch se ₹2-3k mahina, kuch se ₹10k+. Sab wapas nahi milta — par jo galat kata hai uske liye main ladta hu. Pehle free check, phir aap decide karo."

**"Zomato naraz toh nahi hoga? Delist toh nahi karega?"**
> "Bilkul nahi. Galat deduction wapas maangna aapka haq hai. Main quietly karta hu — koi public drama nahi, parity bhi maintain rakhta hu. Aap Zomato pe rehte ho, bas apna paisa wapas paate ho."

**"₹1,500 kyun du jab koi aur free karta hai?"**
> "Free waale sirf *batate* hain ki error hai — phir aap khud Zomato se lado. Main *ladta* hu aapke liye, end-to-end. Aur har mahine prevent bhi karta hu taaki agle mahine kategi hi nahi."

**"Tum 2 saal mein band toh nahi ho jaoge?"**
> "Fair sawaal. Isliye: koi lock-in nahi, monthly billing, data hamesha aapka — ek click mein export. Pasand na aaye toh agle mahine chhod do."

## The demo / proof flow

1. **Run the audit live** — paste their statement into your tool (DeepSeek), show the deductions and the disputable amount on the spot.
2. **Show one real recovered-₹ receipt** from another cafe (get this from your first design partners).
3. **Quote the number for THEIR store** — "Aapke case mein ₹X disputable dikh raha hai."
4. **Don't email — hand them a one-pager** and book the next step in person.

---

## The close

> "Setup mein kuch nahi karna — bas aap mujhe har mahine apni statement forward kar dena (ya main access le lunga). ₹1,500/month, koi lock-in nahi. Pehla dispute main is hafte file kar deta hu. Shuru karein?"

---

## Pricing (how to present it)

- **Flat ₹1,000-1,500/month.** Predictable. Frame against value: *"₹1,500 dekar ₹5,000+ wapas — har mahine."*
- Optional success fee only on large one-time backlog recoveries.
- **Never** lead with "% of recovery" — sounds like you'll inflate numbers, and it shrinks as you fix their leaks.
- The ROI line for the bigger pitch later: *"₹4k vs ₹85k"* — Mehfil costs ₹4k; it replaces ₹35k of staff or recovers ₹50k of lost orders.

---

## Do / Don't

**DO**
- Open with money, every time.
- Approach 3-5 PM only.
- Show real numbers, real receipts.
- Be honest about recovery (some, not all — real net recovery is modest).
- Speak Hindi/Hinglish with older owners.

**DON'T**
- Pitch during lunch/dinner rush.
- Open with "AI" / "POS" / "platform."
- Over-promise ("we'll recover everything / 80%"). Cafes check their own bank statements — one exaggeration kills referrals.
- Bad-mouth Zomato aggressively (retaliation fear is real — frame as "your right," not "fight Zomato").
- Use the debunked stats ("53% take rate," "72% have errors") — they're not real.

---

## The expansion conversation (after Settle earns trust)

Once Settle has recovered money and the owner trusts you, *then* introduce the rest:
> "Ab jab main aapka paisa wapas dila raha hu — ek aur cheez dikhau? Aapka daily profit, kaunsi dish loss mein hai, aur ek AI waiter jo phone pe order leta hai Hindi mein."

Settle gets you in. The platform (Pulse, AI Waiter, audit trail) is what keeps you — and is where the real money is. Don't lead with it; earn the right to it.
