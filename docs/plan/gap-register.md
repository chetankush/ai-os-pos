survived a second agent whose only job was to **refute** it by searching for an existing
implementation. Claims that turned out to be already-built were dropped, so this list is
deliberately conservative.

- **🔴 blocker** — a cafe cannot run a normal day of service, or money is silently wrong or lost.
- **🟠 major** — hit weekly; there is a painful manual workaround.
- **🟡 minor** — polish.

## Staff identity, roles & account recovery

### 🔴 blocker — No password reset, password change, or email change anywhere in the product

**Why it matters.** An owner who forgets the password (or fat-fingers the email at signup, which is auto-confirmed so it is never verified) is permanently locked out of their own POS. There is no self-service path and no admin path in the app: the cafe cannot bill for the rest of the day until someone edits Supabase by hand. Given the owner account is currently the ONLY way anyone gets into the POS, one forgotten password stops the whole outlet.

**Where to fix.** Add apps/web/src/app/(auth)/forgot-password/ + a reset-confirm route calling supabase.auth.resetPasswordForEmail / auth.updateUser, link it from apps/web/src/app/(auth)/login/page.tsx:14, and allow the reset path in the isPublic list at apps/web/src/lib/supabase/middleware.ts:35-43

## Tax correctness: per-item GST, rate-wise summary, session totals

### 🔴 blocker — Per-item GST override is collected in the UI but never applied to a single bill

**Why it matters.** A cafe that sells both restaurant service (5%) and packaged goods or bakery takeaway (18%) types 18 into the item's 'GST override' box, sees 'GST 18%' printed under the item name in the editor, and reasonably believes the bill is now correct. Every such bill is then charged at the cafe-level rate anyway. The error is silent, compounds on every order every day, and surfaces months later as a GST short-payment with interest — on a product whose whole pitch is a clean audit trail.

**Where to fix.** apps/api/src/orders/build.ts:140-175 — the byId map (:140-153) must carry item.gstRateBpOverride, and the single `gstRateBp = gstRateBpFor(cafe.gstMode)` at :173 must become per-line tax with a mixed-rate total. That also forces a decision on order/order_items tax columns (packages/db/src/schema/orders.ts) and on the CGST/SGST split in apps/web/src/app/cafes/[id]/orders/[orderId]/print-views.tsx:209-211, which currently assumes one rate per bill. Until that lands, remove the GST-override field from menu-editor.tsx:594-609 and :898-909 so it stops lying.

**Verifier's note.** CONFIRMED. `gstRateBpOverride` exists in schema (packages/db/src/schema/menu.ts:52), is validated and persisted on create (apps/api/src/routes/menu.ts:37,160) and PATCH (apps/api/src/routes/menu.ts:56), and is rendered back to the owner as 'GST 18%' under the item name (apps/web/src/app/cafes/[id]/menu/menu-editor.tsx:357-370). It is never read by any money math. buildOrder's snapshot map copies only name/price/available/hsn (apps/api/src/orders/build.ts:144-153) and the rate comes solely from the cafe: `const gstRateBp = gstRateBpFor(cafe.gstMode)` (apps/api/src/orders/build.ts:173), a single bill-level rate applied to one taxable base (apps/api/src/orders/build.ts:90-91). A repo-wide grep for `gstRateBpOverride` outside node_modules/dist returns hits only in the schema, types (packages/types/src/domain.ts:66, api.ts:107,121), the menu route/repo, its tests, and the editor — zero hits in orders/build.ts, routes/orders.ts, routes/public.ts, settle, or the print views. Both order paths (counter and QR) call the same buildOrder (apps/api/src/routes/public.ts:111), so both are wrong. Severity confirmed as blocker: silent, permanent, money-wrong output on the product's core GST claim.

### 🟠 major — Per-item GST rate override is collected in the menu editor but silently ignored when the bill is computed

**Why it matters.** The menu editor invites the owner to set an HSN and a GST % per item, saves it, and shows it back on the item card — so the owner reasonably believes a packaged bottled drink or a bakery box is being taxed at its own rate. buildOrder never reads it; every line on every bill gets the single cafe-level rate. The owner under- or over-collects tax on those items indefinitely and only discovers it at filing time. Silent wrong-money is worse than a missing feature, because the UI actively asserts it works.

**Where to fix.** apps/api/src/orders/build.ts:173 — gstRateBp is derived from cafe.gstMode alone. Either apply item.gstRateBpOverride per line (which then requires per-rate tax subtotals on the order, a rate-wise CGST/SGST block on the bill at print-views.tsx:307-315, and a schema change since orders.gstRateBp is a single integer), or remove the field from apps/web/src/app/cafes/[id]/menu/menu-editor.tsx:479-521 so the UI stops promising it.

### 🟠 major — One dine-in tab burns several invoice serials but only one is printed on the paper the guest receives

**Why it matters.** A table that orders three rounds consumes INV/…/000101, 000102 and 000103, but the consolidated bill shows only 000101 as the bill number while the total covers all three. The guest holds one document; GSTR-1 will show three invoices whose individual values do not match anything the customer was given. If a tax officer or a customer ever traces a number back, the paper trail does not line up — which is precisely the audit-trail credibility the product is positioned on.

**Where to fix.** apps/api/src/repositories/orders.ts:110-124 allocates the serial at order creation. Either move invoice-number allocation to bill/settle time (keeping a separate cheap KOT number for kitchen rounds), or print every constituent serial on the consolidated slip at apps/web/src/app/cafes/[id]/tables/sessions/[sessionId]/print/page.tsx:88-91.

### 🟡 minor — The financial year on the invoice number is derived from server-local time instead of IST

**Why it matters.** financialYear(new Date()) reads the container's local clock. On a UTC-hosted API, orders taken between 00:00 and 05:30 IST on 1 April are still 31 March in UTC and get filed into the previous financial year's series, while everything after 05:30 IST starts the new one. Each counter stays internally gapless so nothing breaks operationally, but a handful of invoices land in the wrong FY once a year and the accountant has to unpick them. Every other date reckoning in the codebase already goes through the IST helpers, so this is an inconsistency rather than a design choice.

**Where to fix.** apps/api/src/repositories/orders.ts:115 — pass an IST-adjusted date into financialYear (apps/api/src/orders/build.ts:186), reusing the IST_OFFSET_MINUTES helper at apps/api/src/reports/date-range.ts:9. The same server-local assumption exists in todayStats at apps/api/src/repositories/orders.ts (todayStart via setHours).

### 🟡 minor — The invoice shows no labelled taxable value, and a reprinted KOT is not marked as a reprint

**Why it matters.** Two small paper-trail omissions. Rule 46 expects the taxable value to be stated; the bill prints Subtotal, Discount and charges and leaves the reader to derive it, which is fine for a guest but awkward when an officer reads the slip. Separately, the KOT deliberately isn't print-counted (correct — it's not a financial document), but a re-fired kitchen ticket looks identical to the original, so a cook can double-make a dish when a waiter reprints a slip that was already worked.

**Where to fix.** apps/web/src/app/cafes/[id]/orders/[orderId]/print-views.tsx:296-320 — add a "Taxable value" row before the CGST/SGST block. For the KOT, track a separate in-component reprint flag and stamp "REPRINT" in Kot at :150-187.

## Order amendment, void discipline & lifecycle

### 🔴 blocker — A mis-punched line or a late discount cannot be corrected — the only remedy is cancelling the whole order and re-punching it

**Why it matters.** This happens several times a day, not weekly: a waiter punches Masala Chai instead of Masala Dosa, or the customer produces a discount coupon after the KOT has gone to the kitchen. There is no route to edit, remove or re-price an order line, and no route to apply bill adjustments after creation — the status machine is forward-only with 'cancelled' as the sole escape. So the cashier cancels (burning an invoice serial and orphaning a KOT the kitchen is already cooking), re-punches everything by hand, and re-fires a second KOT.
